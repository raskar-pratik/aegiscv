# Detectors and score semantics

Every finding includes detector, category, attack class, asset, risk, confidence, severity, reason, evidence, status, limitations and action. Status is SUCCESS, WARNING, UNAVAILABLE or FAILED. Negative results have score zero and include execution evidence.

- Exact duplicates: streamed SHA-256, groups of matching bytes. Cannot identify which group member was original.
- Near duplicates: cheap 64-bit dHash and cosine candidates require normalized pixel RMSE and high-frequency residual correlation confirmation. Frozen calibration controls the operating point; configuration may tighten the hash bound. Confirmed edges are recorded; connected-component membership does not claim every transitive pair was verified. Missing or failed calibration qualification makes the detector experimental with zero escalation weight.
- Labels: K nearest visual neighbors, excluding exact byte duplicates. Threshold is neighbor disagreement. Multi-label objects are not analyzed independently.
- OOD: distance to nearest supplied reference embedding. No reference means UNAVAILABLE. Threshold must be calibrated for your encoder/domain.
- Contributors: affected fractions, confidence, severity and detector-quality weights determine each detector contribution; related detectors share an evidence-family maximum. Distinct-family contributions add, bounded at 100. Concentration saturates at 20% of a contributor's images. All findings remain available, including low-reliability evidence. Duplicate-original attribution remains ambiguous. Missing metadata becomes `unknown`.
- Model: byte digest and optional trusted digest comparison; fixed seeded probes and max output change. Softmax confidence assumes logits and is labeled accordingly.
- Neural Cleanse style: bounded Adam optimization of mask/pattern per class, L1 regularization, measured target success, median/MAD anomaly. Small class counts and search budgets limit reliability. Parameters and masks are in evidence JSON.
- Shift: centroid L2, covariance Frobenius norm, RBF MMD squared, label-frequency total variation, and optional absolute mean softmax-confidence difference. Each raw metric is divided by its own configured threshold; the maximum ratio determines heuristic severity only when at least one metric triggers. No p-value or intent attribution is claimed.
- Fusion: fixed-denominator weighted sum of reliability-adjusted category maxima. Data/model/inference/distribution weights are 1/1.2/1.2/1; denominator is always 4.4 by default. Verified model substitution or ledger alteration forces risk 100. Positive evidence additions and coordinate increases cannot reduce risk. Unavailable checks only reduce confidence/coverage. Experimental and review findings can require REVIEW even when global numerical risk is LOW.

Quality multipliers are conservative policy, not fitted probabilities: VERIFIED_MECHANICAL=1, CALIBRATED=1, HEURISTIC=0.6, VALIDATED_EXPERIMENTAL=0.35, EXPERIMENTAL=0.2, UNAVAILABLE=0. Experimental near-duplicate evidence has weight zero. Neural Cleanse retains its raw measured outcomes (5 TP, 1 FP, 2 TN, 1 FN on the historical held-out models) and requires analyst review. Strong backdoor-like evidence requires an anomaly, successful held-out recovery and matching behavioral patch response; it is never automatically called a confirmed backdoor.

Acquisition drift separately measures luminance and Laplacian sharpness using frozen reference/calibration control thresholds. Evidence states OPERATIONAL DISTRIBUTION SHIFT DETECTED without attributing malicious intent; existing embedding thresholds are unchanged. Domain-specific calibration and all operating limits are recorded in core/calibration and the threshold registry.

Severity bands: 0–20 LOW, >20–40 GUARDED, >40–60 MODERATE, >60–80 HIGH, >80 CRITICAL. Default action thresholds are 20 and 80; aggregate action thresholds are configurable. Finding-level actions use the default bands. All ML scores/confidences are heuristic, not calibrated probabilities.

Hardening detectors:

- Spectral: class-centered SVD, squared dominant projection, median/MAD robust score, deterministic rank and class-relative percentile. Default requires at least ten samples, robust score eight, 95th percentile and projection energy 0.001. The energy floor avoids amplifying negligible differences in nearly constant classes; it is uncalibrated and can miss subtle poisons. Defaults operate on L2-normalized embeddings.
- Activation: explicit `get_features` method or eager final-linear input hook, PCA up to five dimensions, deterministic farthest-point two-means. A sufficiently separated minority cluster is evidence, not proof of poisoning. TorchScript hooks and arbitrary model graphs may be unavailable.
- Annotation: retains annotation IDs/files/class associations, checks COCO pixel or YOLO normalized boxes, exact duplicate/conflicting geometry, counts, area ratios, class frequency and contributor links. Malformed/nonfinite boxes become findings and cannot poison canonical JSON with NaNs.
- Repeated pattern: near-constant high-contrast corner patches grouped by corner/quantized color and label association. Watermarks can be false positives.
- Patch probe: independently tests black/white patches at four corners and two image-relative sizes. Concentrated transitions to one target can complement gradient search; benign sensitivity and unseen patch families remain limitations.
- Inversion: sparse-neutral plus dense-bright initializations, adaptive L1 cost, non-target source filtering, minimum training-feasible mask, separate validation success. Only successful target reconstructions enter median/MAD. Undefined MAD/anomaly remains null. Search progress and failure evidence remain visible even when no anomaly is reported.
